PAC-MAN

Controls
- Move: Arrow keys or WASD
- Pause: Esc
- Pause menu: Resume, Restart Game, Exit To Menu

Cheats
- 0: toggle invincibility (ghosts cannot kill you)
- 1: toggle freeze ghosts
- Space: skip the current level instantly

Ghosts
- Blinky (Red): chases you directly
- Pinky (Pink): ambushes 2 cells ahead of your direction
- Inky (Cyan): uses Blinky's position to flank you
- Clyde (Orange): chases you from afar, retreats to his corner when close

Main menu
- Start Game, View Highscores, Instructions, Exit
- Game goal: eat all PacGums to clear the level
- Stay alive and finish all 10 levels to win

Configuration
- Edit config.json to change:
  - highscore_filename
  - levels: list of [width, height] pairs, one per level
  - lives
  - points_per_pacgum
  - points_per_super_pacgum
  - points_per_ghost
  - level_max_time
